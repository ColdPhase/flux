- A space has two roles, Member and Admin. The read-only role (`viewer`) and the explicit deny (`denied`) are gone from the access rules, the grants API, the screens and the database: everyone in a space can work in it, and a person is kept out of a space by removing them from it. A company owner or admin always reaches every space, and **Add me** in Settings › Company now makes them the space's admin. ([#903](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/903))
