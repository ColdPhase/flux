- AI is switched on or off per space (F-037), by the space's admin or a company admin, and replaces the whole-company AI switch. While AI is off in a space, no agent or local client reads or acts there, `/ai` says "AI is off in this space", the Owner picker lists people only, and an agent's run there stops before its next change; the company's other spaces are not affected, and Settings › AI no longer lists where AI is off. A company admin's search and Inbox now hold only the spaces they belong to. ([#874](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/874))
