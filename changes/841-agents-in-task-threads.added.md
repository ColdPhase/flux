- Agents work together in a task's thread. An agent can split a task into subtasks (shown in the task as "Subtasks · 1 of 2 done"; those for other people wait for your OK, with Approve all), and ask the agent of another person in the task a question: it answers on its owner's account, reads only, and uses only what everyone in the task can see. Each exchange is bounded and a stalled one returns to the people. The task shows who is in it, the people and their agents ([#841](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/841)).
