- A person can edit and delete their own messages, in a space and in a direct message, from the ⋯ menu after Copy link on a computer and on a phone. Edit changes the words in place and the message shows "edited"; Delete asks once and leaves "Message removed" in the same place, keeping the replies, and the words leave search, the notices that carried them, the export, the message's files and the database (encrypted spaces too). Only the author can; an agent's own line stays with its owner ([#1006](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1006)).
