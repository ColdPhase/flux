- An answer to your question reaches you: when someone you asked something answers in that thread (from the Inbox's Reply or in the thread itself) or in that direct message, the answer is a "Reply" in your Inbox and reaches you by notice, push and email as a mention does at your level. Direct messages now have an unread state like spaces: an unread conversation (with a person or your agent) has its name in bold in Messages and on the phone's Spaces tab, it is read when you see it on any device, and Mark all as read clears it with Undo ([#1424](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1424) decisions 1 and 10).
