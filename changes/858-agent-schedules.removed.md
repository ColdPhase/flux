- The schedules page in Settings, its sheet with a cron editor and its "post the result in a conversation" choice are gone: schedules belong to an agent, are made by telling it, and their results arrive in its chat. A schedule's answer is no longer posted in a space's conversation, `/ai` in a space no longer makes schedules, and three failures in a row no longer pause one ([#858](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/858)).
