- A local Claude Code or Codex now acts with your rights, like your agents, and has the same "What it may do": Only read, Do it and tell me, or Ask me first. Deleting and other people's things always wait for your OK, and so does everything under Ask me first; you approve in the Inbox, one item per client with Approve all. Only you, signed in, can approve: the client's own token cannot ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859), [#855](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/855)).
- Signing a client in selects every space it may reach, so connecting is one click; you untick what it should not see ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859)).
