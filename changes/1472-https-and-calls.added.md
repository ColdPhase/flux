- HTTPS and calls come with `./flux up`. With an `https` address in `FLUX_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` and `FLUX_TRUSTED_PROXIES` empty it starts a bundled, pinned Caddy that gets and renews the certificate (open ports 80 and 443); your own reverse proxy still works with `FLUX_TRUSTED_PROXIES` set. `FLUX_CALLS=on` adds the media server, writes its key, secret and public address, and prints the ports to open ([#1471](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1471), [#1472](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1472)).
