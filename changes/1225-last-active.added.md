- Space settings › People and Settings › Company › People say when each person was last active in one quiet line under their name: "Active now" (a small mark, within 5 minutes), "Active 12 min ago", "Active 2 h ago", "Yesterday" or "3 Nov". It is account-wide and never says where, is rounded by the server (minutes, hours, then the day in the reader's own zone), is shown only to those who can see the person in that list (a guest sees it only for the people of their own spaces), and costs no extra request and no polling: Flux writes it at most once a minute per person. "Show when I was last active" in Settings › Account, on by default, turns it off and erases the time held. The header's faces list the people active now first ([#1225](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1225), [#1236](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1236)).
