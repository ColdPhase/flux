- Every Undo toast leaves after 10 s (held while the pointer is over it or focus is in it) and at once when you go to another place, so `Z` never undoes something done elsewhere; an answer to an agent's question held for Undo is sent when its toast leaves, while an Accept is sent at once and its Undo takes the acceptance back. While a toast shows, a conversation keeps its end clear of it, so the new task's card and an inline Delete / Keep row are never under it. A task or a decision shows once in a conversation, as one card with who made it, from which message (a link to it) and when; the message carries no chip of it and there is no separate event line; a decision's state is one word ("Proposed"), and its panel says "Decision" once. Beside a docked panel at 1024 px a task card keeps its title and its state inside the card ([#1525](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/pull/1525)).
