- A video someone sends (MP4, WebM or MOV) plays inside Flux when the browser can play it: a larger limit for video (200 MiB by default, `FLUX_VIDEO_MAX_MIB`; other files stay at 5 MiB), the server answers HTTP range requests from disk so a long video starts at once and seeks without loading in full (and Safari plays it), and a poster frame taken in the sender's browser stands for it in the message. No server transcoding; a video the browser cannot play says so and offers Download; in an encrypted space a video is an ordinary 5 MiB file. This change is the server half: the upload, the range route, the poster and the limits ([#1352](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1352)).
