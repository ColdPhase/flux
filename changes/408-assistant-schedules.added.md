- Your assistant can work on a schedule (Settings → Your assistant → On a schedule): a sentence, one project and a time in your own time zone, with Run now, pause, edit, delete with Undo and a history of the last 20 runs. A time Flux could not start within ten minutes is skipped, three failures in a row pause the schedule, and "Let your assistant write your morning summary" puts one line under your Home greeting with the same one push ([#408](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/408)).
