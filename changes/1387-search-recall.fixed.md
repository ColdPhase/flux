- Search ranks by every word you type again: a word after the third of a search counted as the third, so a message holding only a rare later word fell down the list. A word that occurs more often in a message now also ranks it higher, still counted only among what you may read; on the research bench, plain search finds 0.617 of the answers in its first ten instead of 0.467, and with the optional local embeddings 0.700 instead of 0.683 ([#1387](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1387)).
