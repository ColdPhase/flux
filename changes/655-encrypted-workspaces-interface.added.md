- People can make an encrypted space from Home → Details → People → New space: a generated six-word unlock password or their own, a recovery code shown once to print, and no AI in it, for good. An encrypted space that waits to be unlocked shows as one row ("Waiting to be unlocked") and as a locked panel at every address inside it; owners and admins unlock it there, and open tabs follow the lock and unlock without a reload. People → Encryption lets owners and admins change their unlock password, lock the space, let another admin set a first password and make a new recovery code, and says what removing an owner or admin changes ([#655](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/655)).
