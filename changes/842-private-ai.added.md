- `/ai` works in every conversation: a space's conversation and its threads, a task's thread and a direct message. The answer is yours alone, in a card with Dismiss and Show everyone, which posts it as your own message, "Ada · via Assistant". It uses only what the people of that conversation can see; a guest has no `/ai`. In a direct message both people see one line, "Ada used AI here (Claude)" ([#842](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/842)).
- Each of your agents has a private chat in Messages with a box to write in: it answers there, remembers what was said and "remember that …" adds to its memory. In its chat an agent reads like you and changes nothing ([#842](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/842)).
