- A space or a conversation with AI off keeps its meaning search: local embeddings (`FLUX_EMBEDDINGS=on`) are an index like the word search, not an AI company, so those places are embedded like any other and people's search there stays hybrid, while agents are kept out by each search's access check. With embeddings on, the worker embeds those spaces in the background after the upgrade ([#1591](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1591)).
