- In a call, the microphone has a small arrow (and ⋯ has a row) that opens Audio settings: choose the microphone and the speakers or headphones, see a level meter before you turn the microphone on, set the microphone volume (0 to 200 %) and what you hear overall, switch noise, echo and volume handling, and play a test sound. Each person's face has their own Volume (0 to 200 %) and Mute for me, which only you hear. Everything is remembered on this device; a device that disappears falls back to the default and says so; an iPhone says to use its volume buttons ([#1244](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1244)).
