- A picture is decoded and written again by the server before it is stored, whoever sent it (a person's upload, a picture an agent attaches from the web), and a file in which decoders would disagree about the picture is refused: a WebP with a second image chunk, a canvas that is not its image's size, an animation frame outside its canvas. A 52-byte WebP could be checked as 1×1 and drawn at 16383×16383 by every browser, which crashed the tab of everyone who opened the conversation. A picture is at most 8,000 pixels on a side and 40 million pixels, and the app draws inline only a picture within that, by every size its headers claim ([#1343](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1343)).
