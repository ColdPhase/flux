- Signing in Claude Code, Codex or another local client is one click: its sign-in opens one page that says who asks and where access goes, and **Allow** is the whole setup. Allow makes the client's agent (yours) and its connection at "Do it and tell me", or finds the one the client already has; **Deny** and merely looking at the page make nothing. The client then acts with your rights, in the spaces where AI is on, and no more ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859)).
- Disconnect ends a client's access at once and closes what it had waiting; signing in again makes a new connection and gives the client's earlier agent back ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859)).
- A client's undo of a task it made waits for your OK like any deletion, and an Approve that comes after the claim or session it was made under has ended says so in plain words instead of applying ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859)).
