- The Map is an infinite canvas: drag empty space or use Move around to pan, zoom around the pointer, and use the minimap and Fit; thoughts can sit at negative positions. The List is the map's tree: a thought sits under the thought its connection was drawn from, with "also under …" for its other parents, and Tab / Shift+Tab move the connection. Create task on the Map opens the Create window with the selected thoughts as its sources ([#554](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/pull/554), [#509](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/509), [#510](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/510)).
