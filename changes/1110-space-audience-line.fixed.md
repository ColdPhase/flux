- A space's people line no longer says "Only you" or "· only you two", which was false because the company's owners and admins can open every space; it names the people the space was added to, and a company owner or admin who was not added is not called "you". The space's details say once who else can open it, and "Add to a Wiki page" is no longer written twice under the panel's heading. An address an identity provider reports with a decomposed letter is looked up as the same mailbox when a pending seat is claimed and a standing profile is read; sign-up, sign-in and reset take only ASCII addresses and answer 400 to any other ([#1110](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1110)).
