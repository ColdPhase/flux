- A decision is called "Decision" everywhere: the Inbox card, the return summary and the notification no longer say "rule" or "proposal". "Replaces" and "In the Wiki" are plain sentences, drawn only when they apply: "This replaces the decision … (12 Oct).", "This was replaced by the decision …" and "Shown on the Wiki page …". Accepting a decision that replaces another only marks the old one replaced ([#871](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/871)).
- Words about who can see a place appear only when the audience differs from the place's default. A space's conversation, its threads, a task, a space's map and a direct message say nothing under the box; a private map still says "Only you", and a private answer still says who sees it. Header subtitles that described a page are gone, "Who can see this" in the header's ⋯ menu is "People", and "Came from" on a task is "From" ([#871](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/871)).
- A place's panel has one name on every device: "Space details" in a space and "Message info" in a direct message. Home, My map and My tasks have no Details ([#871](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/871)).
