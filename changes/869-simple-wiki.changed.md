- The Wiki is two columns, the sidebar and the page: the page is edited where it is read, and versions save by themselves while you write and when you leave. The title is the page switcher (Find a page, + New page), ≡ lists the page's headings, and ⋯ holds History (compare and Restore), Copy link, Export, Import a Markdown file and Rename page. A task opened from a page opens over it ([#869](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/869)).
