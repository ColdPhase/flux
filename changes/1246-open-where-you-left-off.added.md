- Every conversation opens where you left off: a space, a direct message, a thread, a task's discussion and an agent's chat. It opens on its first new message with a quiet "New" divider above it (it said "Unread" in a space), or at its end when nothing is new. One you have never opened (a new direct message with 60 messages) opens at its first message with the divider above it. While you read above the end, what arrives is counted in "↓ 3 new" at the foot (it listed messages and tasks separately in a space); a click, a tap or End takes you to the end, and nothing moves under you ([#1246](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1246)).
