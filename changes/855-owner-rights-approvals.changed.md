- An agent now has exactly its owner's rights, and it loses them the moment its owner does. Each agent has one "What it may do": Only read, Do it and tell me (the default) or Ask me first. Deleting and other people's things always wait for your OK, and only you, signed in, can give it ([#855](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/855)).
- Undo now sits on the agent's message, next to one line that says what it changed, and the line then reads "Undone by Ada". A deletion asks Delete or Keep, a task for someone else is a proposal with Approve, Edit and Skip plus Approve all, and the Inbox shows one item per agent run. Nothing that waits expires ([#855](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/855)).
- MCP tool contract 2: the `flux://policy/{projectId}/{revision}` resource and bootstrap's `trusted.approvedPolicy` are gone, because an agent now has its owner's rights. Local clients get `AGENT_ONLY_READS`, `AGENT_ASKS_FIRST` or `AGENT_CHANGE_WAITS` instead of a change that would have to wait ([#855](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/855)).
