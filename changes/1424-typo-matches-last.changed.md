- In people's search a match with a typo shows only when nothing else matches: a title with one typo in a word of four letters or more is listed only when no hit holds any of the searched words whole, so "price list" lists the price list and no longer a person named "Lis". "Whole" counts the word's other forms (plural, accents, a hyphen joined) and a little word such as "the", and a hit that holds one counts even where Search leaves it out for holding only little words. "liss" alone still finds "Lis" ([#1424](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1424) decision H).
