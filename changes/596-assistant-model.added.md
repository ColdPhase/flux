- You choose your assistant's model when you turn it on, and change it from Settings or from the ask bar and the assistant's panel in a conversation. It is one model everywhere: a change applies from your next request, a request already working keeps its model, and each answer's footer names the model that answered. A model your plan or key does not offer is refused in plain words and the previous one stays ([#596](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/596)).
