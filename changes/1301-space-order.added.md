- The Spaces list is in your own order, the same on every device and in every tab: drag a space to move it (a long press, then a drag, on a touch screen), or use ⋯ › Move up and Move down. Everyone stays first and a new space goes to the end. A space with unread messages is bold, and the Spaces heading's ⋯ and the Inbox's ⋯ have "Mark all as read", with Undo, which leaves what needs your action in the Inbox ([#1301](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/pull/1301)).
