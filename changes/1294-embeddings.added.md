- Operators with a larger server can turn on an optional local embedding container (`FLUX_EMBEDDINGS=on`, about 3.2 GiB of RAM idle): search then also ranks by meaning, fusing the nearest vectors with BM25. It is off by default and stores nothing while off; encrypted spaces and spaces with AI off are never embedded, and nothing is embedded through a person's own AI connection.
