- Owners and admins can invite someone with a one-time link from a workspace's People: choose member or guest (admin for owners), optionally lock it to one email address, and pick how long it works (1, 7 or 30 days). The person opens the link, signs in or creates an account, and joins with that role. Pending links are listed with Copy and Revoke; a link works once, and Flux sends no email itself. In an encrypted workspace an admin who joins gets an unlock password only from someone who has one ([F-035](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/blob/main/docs/development/access-policy.md#invite-links-f-035), [#763](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/763)).
