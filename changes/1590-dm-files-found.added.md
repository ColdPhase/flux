- Files in a direct message are found by the people in it: a file's name and text (a PDF's or a document's and, with `FLUX_FILE_OCR=on`, a picture's or a scan's) are found by ⌘K and the search API, only by people who can read that conversation now, and `/ai` there reads them (a model that sees pictures looks at them) with the "used AI here" line as always. An encrypted space's files are never read; the files already sent are read in the background after the upgrade ([#1590](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/1590)).
