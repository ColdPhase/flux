- New space is one window with a name, the people (chosen from the company) and an AI switch that starts on; you are the space's admin. Each space has settings under its header's ⋯ menu: its name, its people with Admin and Member roles, the AI switch, Leave space and, for admins, Delete space, which asks first and names the space. A company admin can delete any space from Settings › Company, but not Everyone; deleting a space also removes its files from the server. A space always keeps an admin: nobody leaves, steps down or is removed while they are its last admin. ([#874](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/874))
