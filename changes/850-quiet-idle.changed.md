- An idle server is quieter: its worker looks at its queues once a minute instead of every 30 seconds when nothing has woken it, and its other housekeeping looks less often too, so a Flux nobody is using makes about 95 database statements a minute, down from 122. A delayed or retried job can start up to a minute late instead of half a minute; a job sent now still starts at once ([#850](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/850)).
