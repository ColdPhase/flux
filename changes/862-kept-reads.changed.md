- Moving between places asks the server less: the project, its people, the members, the space's docs, what waits for you and the agents' unread dots are kept for a moment, so the next view shows them without asking again. Anything that could change them (a change you make, a live update, signing out) forgets them, and a refresh on focus still reads. Going from Home to the Inbox now costs one request instead of four, and entering a space 13 instead of 20 ([#862](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/862)).
