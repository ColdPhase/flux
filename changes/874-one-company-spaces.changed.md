- One Flux is one company (F-037): the first account sets the company up, as its owner, with the default space Everyone that holds the whole company. With single sign-on everyone the identity provider lets in joins on their first sign-in; without it, company admins invite people with links. Projects are now called spaces, in one flat list in the sidebar (Home, Inbox, My tasks, My map, Spaces, Messages); a person sees only the spaces they were added to, everyone but a guest starts spaces, and a space's creator is its first admin. Settings › Company replaces People, with the company's name and its people. There is no way to create or switch workspaces. ([#874](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/874)) Settings › Company also lists every space, where an owner or admin adds themselves, and people who left or were removed, with Restore; a company admin's sidebar holds only the spaces they belong to. The company's name is on the sign-in page and in email.
