- Switching between a project's Conversation, Map, Tasks and Wiki no longer flashes: the old view stays whole and at full strength until the new one has painted and then dissolves into it (it used to fade as see-through ink over the new view, or while the new view was still empty), in light and dark, on computer and phone, and with reduced motion it simply switches. The computer's sidebar now folds to its rail and unfolds with a short size change instead of snapping, with the toggle staying where it is and keeping focus ([#719](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/719), [#720](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/720)).
