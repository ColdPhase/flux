# Research a Flux decision

Read `AGENTS.md`, the milestone/task brief, relevant foundation sections, and
[the research guide](../../../docs/product/research.md). Use the
[decision register](../../../docs/product/decisions.md) to identify what is open.

- Name the persona, situation, desired outcome and uncertainty that could change
  the recommendation. Reuse relevant current research; match depth to consequence.
- Compare actual alternatives and seek evidence that could weaken the hypothesis.
  Use primary documentation for technical claims. Separate observations, vendor
  claims, founder preference, and inference, with dates and versions as needed.
- Own-AI research must separate official external agents, embedded execution,
  and API/local models: capability, authorization, billing and implementation
  status are different questions. Recheck provider terms before making promises.
- Return a defensible recommendation, relevant alternatives, costs, limitations,
  open decision owner, and a condition for reconsideration. A source list alone
  is not the result; do not fabricate interviews or product tests.
- Record reusable findings with the relevant domain docs. A peer accepts the
  research quality; founder/owner acceptance of the decision stays explicit.
- If a source or decision blocks one part, document the gap, ask the peer for
  concrete help, and continue independent research or another milestone task.

An application stack remains a proposal until accepted. The founder's Docker
environment requirement already applies to technology comparisons and experiments.
