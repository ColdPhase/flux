# Design a Flux working surface

Read `AGENTS.md`, [the design guide](../../../docs/design/README.md), the task
brief, current decisions, and foundation sections 10 / 17 D1–D4 / 21.

- Identify the persona, job, surface, primary action, difficult states, references,
  and accepted constraints. The prototype, colors, fonts and third-party skill
  defaults are inspiration until a direction is accepted.
- For initial direction compare at least two coherent variants on the same
  realistic scenario. For a local correction preserve unrelated good elements
  and the accepted direction. Separate interaction references from visual ones.
- Render the whole view at consistent viewport and 100% zoom. Inspect density,
  hierarchy, meaningful content, long names and the open agent panel. Improve
  spacing/grouping rather than scaling or shrinking the entire interface.
- Use `flux-review-visual` through an available independent reviewer with a fresh
  neutral brief. Start with at most two focused correction rounds; further work
  must address a material remaining problem, not chase a score.
- Separately verify real interaction, keyboard/focus, contrast, narrow layouts,
  enlarged text and applicable data paths in the containerized application.
  Mark unavailable checks or self-review honestly.
- Hand off rendered variants, recommendation, decision status, observed problems
  and evidence. Once accepted, update the design record and real tokens/components
  together. Do not turn an unaccepted experiment into a global design rule.
