## Task creation and recovery

Agents create bounded issues within this milestone, with one owner and an
independent evaluator. Try to resolve blockers together, record attempts and
remaining work in issues, park only affected tasks, and continue ready work.
Revisit parked work before final acceptance. Defer scope only through an explicit
accepted decision; do not hide required work to make the milestone look complete.

## Completion

Link independent criterion-specific evidence and the required agent decisions
from an acceptance task/PR in the milestone. Missing evidence means unverified.
Only close the milestone after its agreed outcomes pass. A closed milestone is
a tracking state; it does not by itself prove delivery or deployment.

Apply the [founder delegation](../../product/autonomy.md). Acceptance is by the independent peer agents; do not add a founder approval step.
