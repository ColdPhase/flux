### Completed and remaining criteria

List criterion IDs with a brief, factual state. Link decisions and related tasks.

### Verification

Record commands or scenarios actually run, their outcomes, and evidence links.
List pending, failing, or unavailable checks explicitly.

### Next action

State the next concrete step, dependency or question, and who can unblock it.
Record workspace/environment details needed to reproduce the checks. Keep secrets
and machine-private session data out of this shared report.

### Recovery notes

Link the existing PR and the last good checkpoint. Describe any unpushed or
uncommitted work and its location honestly; the peer cannot recover local-only
work until it is made available. Record whether the active claim was released.
