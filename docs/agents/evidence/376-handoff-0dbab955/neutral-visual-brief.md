# Neutral visual brief: Agents hand-off (partial #347)

A project collaborator needs to inspect project agents, understand their owner and current work, see what a selected agent may do, and hand a task to an agent after seeing its permissions. Assess the Agents list, selected details, policy copy, and two-step hand-off on desktop and phone against the fixed Prostota direction (F-026).

Read this brief, [manifest.json](manifest.json), the mapped screenshots listed there, and these design references: [final design guide](../../../design/final/README.md), [desktop Agents](../../../design/final/screens/desktop-agents.webp), [desktop hand-off](../../../design/final/screens/desktop-handoff.webp), [phone Agents](../../../design/final/screens/phone-agents.webp), [phone agent details](../../../design/final/screens/phone-agentdetail.webp), and [phone hand-off](../../../design/final/screens/phone-handoff.webp).

The manifest maps the 40 frames reviewed for AC-1, AC-2, AC-5, C-1, C-3 and C-4, plus four Chromium frames after scrolling the enlarged-text permission summary. It records source and stored-image hashes, engine, viewport, theme and scenario. Images are lossless WebP and were pixel-verified against their PNG sources.

Assess hierarchy, useful density, state and identity clarity, primary action, readable permission copy, text containment and consistency across widths and themes. Report only material visible issues, with frame, symptom, user consequence and severity; distinguish observations from preferences and say what should be preserved. Keep interaction, persistence, authority, accessibility and physical-device conclusions out of a screenshot-only verdict. Current-head live tests are separate evidence in [verification.md](verification.md).

This PR is a partial delivery. Stop/questions, Message, richer A7, phone shell and integrated #347 acceptance remain outside this slice. Do not assess or claim those as delivered here. This is a neutral brief, not an expected verdict or a summary of the author's findings.
