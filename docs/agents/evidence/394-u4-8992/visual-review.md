# Independent visual review — PR #394 U4

**Result: visual pass** for the supplied current-head screenshots at `8992ad2d8315f2e481150fac6e56a5ec6bedae0d`. This assessment was made by a fresh-context Luna evaluator, independent of the capture operator.

The desktop detail panel gives the task title and read-only undo status clear priority; the source, undo actor/date, and activity entries remain grouped in the same panel. In the conversation, the original task title is repeated in the chronological notice, and the undo event names Ari Task author (“you”) and shows 3:58 PM. At phone width the notice wraps into a compact, legible two-line event with the task title contained in its card. The Details sheet keeps the title, status, and undo explanation visible above its fold and signals more content with “Drag up for more.” The creator entry is lower in the activity history, so it is not initially visible in the phone screenshot; the screenshot does not establish whether the full activity and retained conversation are usable after scrolling. No material visible hierarchy, density, wrapping, containment, or readability issue merits a finding.

Preserve the monochrome event rows, explicit “read-only history” status, calm task-detail grouping, and compact linked task card. This is a visual assessment only: interaction, accessibility, and persistence remain unverified from images.

The evaluator inspected six captures from the maintained test. Five unique images are stored here as pixel-identical lossless WebP; the phone keyboard-history capture has the same SHA-256 as the phone Details capture and is represented by that same image. The image mapping and source PNG/WebP hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).
