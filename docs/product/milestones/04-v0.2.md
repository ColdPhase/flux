# Milestone v0.2 — simple AI, a simpler and faster Flux, security

Created 2026-11-02 after the v0.1.0 release and reshaped 2026-11-03 by founder direction (Hubert /
PelikanFix16). GitHub milestone:
[v0.2 — simple AI, a simpler and faster Flux, security](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/milestone/6).
It absorbs the closed [v0.1.1](03-v0.1.1.md); arm64 moved to [v0.3](05-v0.3.md).

**Direction:** [#851](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/851) (simple AI),
[#861](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/861) (a simpler Flux) and
[#874](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/874) (one Flux per company, with Spaces), recorded as
[F-036 to F-038](../decisions.md#f-036-to-f-038-the-v02-direction-2026-11-03). Flux became a combine
harvester instead of a tool that speeds work up; v0.2 makes it simple, fast and calm. Flux connects
people, and AI only helps.

## Outcomes

1. **Decisions and mockups first.** The decision record and the rewritten AI contracts
   ([#852](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/852)) pass an independent review before the
   implementation issues start. Hubert reviews the mockups of the AI screens
   ([#853](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/853)) and of the simpler Flux, including the
   Settings direction ([#864](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/864)), before UI work ships;
   #864 then updates `docs/design/final/`.
2. **Simple AI: everything is an agent** ([agents](../assistant.md)). One Connect AI per person
   with several connections ([#854](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/854)); the owner's
   rights, a per-agent "What it may do", Undo and the per-space AI switch
   ([#855](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/855)); agents on the server that take tasks
   through their owner ([#856](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/856)); agents with a name,
   instructions, model and memory ([#857](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/857)); schedules
   from a sentence, with reactions and background suggestions removed
   ([#858](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/858)); local Claude Code and Codex with the
   person's rights ([#859](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/859)); server capacity without a
   fixed cap ([#860](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/860)); agents together in a task
   ([#841](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/841)); `/ai` everywhere, answered privately
   ([#842](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/842)); no Flux limits
   ([#843](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/843)); a task keeps the message it came from
   ([#844](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/844)).
3. **One Flux is one company, with Spaces** ([#874](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/874)):
   one flat list of Spaces, per-space people, AI switch and encryption, SSO joining and the
   Everyone space.
4. **A simpler Flux** ([#861](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/861)): one place for what
   needs you and the new Home ([#865](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/865)); Thought and
   Map ([#866](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/866)); one Create and three message actions
   ([#867](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/867)); Tasks, statuses and task details
   ([#868](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/868)); a simple Wiki
   ([#869](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/869)); one search
   ([#870](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/870)); calmer words
   ([#871](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/871)); Settings, Notifications and the account
   menu ([#872](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/872)); the space header and views
   ([#873](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/873)).
5. **Fast and light.** Flux feels quick on a VPS with 2 vCPU and 4 GB RAM for a team of a few
   people with a few server agents: baseline, targets, fixes and a CPU-limited regression phase
   ([#862](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/862)), with
   [#850](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/850),
   [#827](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/827) and
   [#828](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/828). Hubert's targets for that host
   (2026-11-03, [F-038](../decisions.md#f-038--a-simpler-flux)): idle at most 2% CPU and 100
   database statements a minute; an open tab at most 4 requests a minute; a navigation at most 8
   requests with no repeats and content within 500 ms; server p95 at most 50 ms; at most 250 KB
   on a first visit before content; an image of at most 250 MB; a restart in at most 5 s. Your own
   actions show at once with a "Couldn't save · Try again" rollback, and other people's changes
   arrive as a push over the existing live connection instead of 15–20 s polling, with one
   catch-up fetch after a reconnect. Flux computes from memory how many agents run at once (about
   2 on 4 GB) and the rest wait ([#860](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/860)). Coherent
   motion under one rule set ([#863](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/863), with Kreska
   [#849](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/849)).
6. **Bugs:** [#846](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/846) (install on a slow database),
   [#847](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/847) (SSO-only sign-in form) and
   [#848](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/848) (creating an encrypted space).
7. **Security.** Scanning runs alongside the work: dependency updates and advisories
   ([#834](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/834)), release image scanning
   ([#835](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/835)) and CodeQL
   ([#836](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/836)); PR Actions stay light, and heavy scans run
   on the release pipeline or a schedule. Last, on the new code: an independent audit against a
   reviewed threat model and OWASP ASVS L2 ([#837](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/837)),
   DAST in Docker ([#838](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/838)), an authorization and
   isolation pentest ([#839](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/839)) and signed release
   images ([#840](https://github.com/ColdPhase/flux/issues/840)). P1 and P2 findings are fixed before
   v0.2.

## Boundaries

- No data migration: Flux is pre-release, with no installations to convert.
- The AI safety rules in [agents](../assistant.md) and [where agents run](../ai-modes.md) hold for
  every slice: owner's rights, owner-only prompting, deletions and other people's things wait,
  Undo, no plan tokens, no impersonation, encrypted spaces without AI.
- The feature issues carry draft acceptance criteria; agents refine them in planning
  ([plan a task](../../agents/workflow.md)) within F-036 to F-038.
