C-1 Guest visibility of archived items in exports

Ari, Nia, Jo · 9 messages · 1 open question
  1. AR
    AriDesignerSep 20, 10:12

    Support case from Halden Co M-1: a guest opened the scheduled XLSX export and saw the titles of three archived tasks they never had access to. The archive view hides them correctly — the export query doesn't check guest visibility at all.

  2. NI
    NiaEngineerSep 20, 10:31

    Confirmed. The export runs with the permissions of the member who scheduled it, not the person receiving it. D-1 says “include archived items for members”, and a scheduled export inherits that — so guests get whatever the member could see.

    It also affects items archived after the export was scheduled: the item list is frozen at scheduling time. The spec that describes this was M-3 missing.

  3. AR
    AriDesignerSep 21, 14:40

    Proposal: exclude every item the recipient cannot open, and evaluate that when the file is generated, not when it is scheduled. That covers late archiving too. Members keep today's behaviour.
